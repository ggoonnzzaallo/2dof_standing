The two order totals visible in screenshots sum to **$52.41**, including the two spare batteries, switch and leads. Other delivered costs are unknown, so the actual total against the original $100 target is **not yet verified**. Personal details from receipts are deliberately omitted here.

No external IMU, Raspberry Pi, PCA9685, boost converter or separate charger is in this revision. A bulk capacitor or signal resistors are **diagnostic options only** if the assembled robot exhibits measured voltage dips or signal back-powering; do not add them preemptively. The servo red wires must be fed from the battery through the Pololu switch, **never** from XIAO 3V3 or VBUS. See [power and charging plan](HARDWARE.md).

The PTK family is specified by the [manufacturer's related MG-D page](https://www.ptkhobby.com/en/sys-pd/234.html) for about 5–8.4 V, whereas a 1S cell spans about 4.2 V down to the planned 3.6 V cutoff. The [reference author](https://homemadegarbage.com/rl13) reports actual 3.7 V operation without boost. That makes direct 1S a reasonable experiment for this mechanism, **not a rated operating condition or guaranteed recovery margin**. Incoming bench measurements decide whether the design can stay boost-free.
