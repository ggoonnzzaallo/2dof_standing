# CAD and printable parts

The robot combines the user's final Onshape arm with a generated under-base electronics enclosure.

| Part of the robot | Authoritative files and guide |
|---|---|
| Arm, servo placements and hinge axes | [Final Onshape import](onshape_import/README.md) |
| Electronics cup | [C3 enclosure](electronics_pod/README.md): tighter USB opening and battery against the side wall |
| Lid | Same fit-approved C2 lid; two M2×10 screws into plastic |
| Print package | [C3 ZIP](electronics_pod/rev_c3_print_fit.zip) · [print/fit guide](electronics_pod/PRINT_FIT_CHECK.md) |
| Training geometry and joint motion | [Current C3 simulation](../sim/README.md) |

Headerless XIAO, battery and one Pololu master switch sit inside the enclosure. The enclosure guide contains layouts, previews and source-generation details. C2 passed the recorded fit check; the latest C3 cup changes are tracked separately.

The older root-level generated arm files, viewer and fit coupons are retained as a [historical concept](GENERATED_CONCEPT.md). They are not the as-built arm or current training geometry.
