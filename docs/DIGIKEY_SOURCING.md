The six DigiKey line items total **$43.13 before tax, tariffs and freight** at the displayed quantities and prices. That buys 100 of each fastener, which is more expensive and wasteful than the $7.23 25-set fastener kit in the main BOM. The switch's Marketplace shipping could erase any checkout convenience. If avoiding excess fasteners matters, keep the original kit or select a smaller compatible DigiKey fastener assortment after checking its exact contents.

The remaining off-site parts are:

- **Two FEETECH FT90M servos from AliExpress.** No exact AliExpress listing has been verified; follow the [model, 3–8.4 V rating, 20T horn and screw checks](ALIEXPRESS_SOURCING.md). The AliExpress servo price is not included in the $43.13 figure. Do not substitute a nominal 4.8–6 V SG90 to achieve a one-store cart.
- **One high-discharge 1S battery.** The [E-flite EFLB5001S25 at Vortex Hobbies](https://vortexhobbies.com/flite-500mah-37v-25c-lipo-battery-eflb5001s25-p-70086.html) is the current 500 mAh, 25C, JST-RCY, CAD-sized BOM pack at $11.99. DigiKey does list 500 mAh packs such as [TinyCircuits ASR00035](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/tinycircuits/ASR00035/9808767), but that page specifies a **JST-SH connector and different 31 × 20 × 9.5 mm dimensions** and does not establish the discharge rating needed for two loaded servos. It is **not an approved drop-in substitute**. No matching high-discharge DigiKey pack has been verified.
- **One battery mating lead.** The exact [Pololu 2181 male JST-RCY lead](https://www.pololu.com/product/2181) remains the qualified 20-AWG baseline. DigiKey's [JST A02SYP22KR254SS prewired RCY plug](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/jst-sales-america-inc/A02SYP22KR254SS/9951116) is currently not stocked and has a 1,000-piece minimum. Vortex lists an [Injora XT30-to-male-JST-RCY adapter](https://vortexhobbies.com/injora-xt30-jst-rcy-jst-ph20-trx-style-ec3-battery-adapter-cab-injxt30-jst-ph2-p-257990.html) at $2.99; its XT30 end could be removed, but the listed 60 mm lead has **no published wire gauge**, so it is only a candidate pending wire/connector verification. A generic item titled simply “JST” may use a different connector; match the battery's actual JST-RCY housing and check polarity before soldering.

Thus a **DigiKey + AliExpress + Vortex** three-website cart is plausible **if** the Vortex mating lead passes the connector, gauge and polarity check. The parts with published prices would total **$58.11 before the two AliExpress servos, tax, tariffs and shipping** ($43.13 DigiKey + $11.99 battery + $2.99 candidate lead). This is not a verified delivered price or a complete approved checkout. If the lead is unsuitable, buy the exact Pololu 2181; it adds a vendor unless sourced together with the switch directly from Pololu. For the fewest actual shipments, compare final carts rather than website count.
