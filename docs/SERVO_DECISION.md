# Servo choice: joint readback versus smart-servo precision

**Decision update, 1 October 2026:** The user ordered **two PTK 7465 MG** servos, matching the [reference self-righting robot](https://homemadegarbage.com/rl13), whose author confirms operation from one 3.7 V LiPo without boost. The earlier FT90M recommendation and comparison below are retained as design history; FT90M is **not** the chosen or ordered servo. The CAD and MuJoCo geometry now use provisional PTK dimensions, while actual non-W dimensions, horn fit and one-cell servo dynamics still require incoming inspection and bench measurement.

The SO-100 arm uses **FEETECH STS3215** serial bus servos. They are a different actuator class from a three-wire RC servo or the four-wire FT90M-FB. The choice is about actuator control and joint observability, not cable count.

| Choice | What the controller receives | Power and physical fit | Rev A implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| FT90M, superseded candidate | PWM angle command only. The servo's internal potentiometer closes its own loop; XIAO cannot read actual angle. | FEETECH rates it for 3–8.4 V, so a 1S cell can power it directly. Micro-servo size. | Historical datasheet-supported option; not purchased for Rev A. |
| PTK 7465 MG, ordered Rev A | PWM angle command only; no joint-angle telemetry exposed to XIAO. | Reference builder reports 3.7 V success, but [related PTK MG-D specifications](https://www.ptkhobby.com/en/sys-pd/234.html) start around 5 V; direct 1S is outside published range. Provisional body envelope is 23.9 × 12 mm with 25T ~5 mm spline. | Better evidence for this particular recovery mechanism, subject to measurements across the cell voltage range and OEM horn fit. |
| FT90M-FB, optional substitution | PWM command plus a separate **analog angle feedback** wire. No digital speed/current/temperature reports. | FEETECH rates it for 3–8.4 V, with a 23.2 × 12 × 25.5 mm case and 12.5 g weight. Same 1S supply concept; two ADC inputs and wiring changes are needed to consume feedback. | Useful if real joint angle is valuable to the policy. It does **not** make the actuator's gears, internal loop, or output precision better merely by exposing the signal. |
| STS3215, SO-100 actuator | Half-duplex TTL serial command and digital reports of position, speed, load, voltage, current, and temperature. Its magnetic encoder has 12-bit electronic resolution (0.088° per count), and the sheet specifies ≤0.5° gear backlash versus ≤2° for the FT90M. Resolution and backlash specifications are **not** guaranteed loaded positioning accuracy. | Manufacturer rates 4–7.4 V, with undervoltage protection below 4 V; 45.2 × 24.7 × 35 mm, about 55 g **each**. Two alone weigh about 110 g before structure, battery and controller. | Requires redesigned CAD, higher-power battery architecture (or suitable boost), bus interface, and a new power/brownout analysis. Do not substitute into the existing BOM or printed mounts. |

The [SO-100 parts list](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100/blob/main/SO100.md) lists STS3215 servos at about $14–15 each and a separate motor-control board. That price is a reference-project estimate, not a delivered US quote. The [STS3215 manufacturer's sheet](https://files.seeedstudio.com/products/Feetech/108090023_STS3215-C001_Datasheet.pdf) gives the electrical, mechanical and telemetry details above. Its 4 V minimum and below-4 V protection leave almost no useful margin on a 1S LiPo: a charged cell starts at 4.2 V, and most of its discharge occurs below 4 V. The SO-100 uses an external power supply; its actuator choice should not be transplanted into this onboard 1S design without redesigning the power system.

**Historical decision, superseded:** The first version chose FT90M as a datasheet-supported 1S baseline and considered FT90M-FB if readback were worth two extra wires. The current purchased PTK servos remain ordinary three-wire PWM servos with no exposed joint-angle feedback. Bench-test travel, loaded response and self-righting with a scripted maneuver before treating the selected hardware as validated. A future smart-servo revision would be a different mechanical and electrical design.

The [FT90M specification](https://evelta.com/content/datasheets/501-FT90M.pdf) and [FT90M-FB product page](https://www.feetechrc.com/2kg-cm-digital-steering-gear-ft90m-fb.html) provide the two micro-servo operating ranges and dimensions. If we use joint-angle observations, update the MuJoCo policy and onboard firmware together; do not give the simulated policy perfect joint position while the physical policy sees only prior commands.
